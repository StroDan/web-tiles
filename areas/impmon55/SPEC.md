# Floating Toad

A pixel-art toad floats around the window, bouncing off the edges and bobbing gently up and
down.

- The toad is drawn in code from a 16 by 12 grid of letters (one letter per pixel, one colour
  per letter) and shown 8 times bigger, with the pixels kept sharp. No image files.
- A click moves the toad so it is centred on the pointer.
- The arrow keys add speed in that direction. The toad keeps moving and bounces off the
  edges.
- The page fills the whole window and follows its size.
