# Bubbles

Soft, colorful bubbles float up the screen. Pop them.

- 30 bubbles at the start, at random positions and sizes. Each rises at its own speed and
  sways gently side to side.
- A bubble that floats off the top comes back in at the bottom.
- Clicking a bubble pops it: it bursts into a short ring that fades out.
- Clicking empty space blows 5 new bubbles from the pointer.
- Space adds 10 bubbles along the bottom.
- C clears every bubble.
- The bubbles fill the whole window and follow its size.
- A counter in the corner shows how many bubbles have been popped.
